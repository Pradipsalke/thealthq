The ALT HQ model

Product Mindset. Startup Speed. Enterprise Scale.

Three ideas anchor the journey from delivery centre to alternate headquarters.

01

Product Mindset

Shift from a service-centric to a product-centric operating model, with true end-to-end ownership of business outcomes.

02

Startup Speed

Move beyond process optimisation to measurable innovation, partnering with India's startup ecosystem to respond quickly to opportunity.

03

Enterprise Scale

Build leadership, governance and metrics tied to business outcomes, so the centre earns larger, global responsibilities.

AI runs through all three. The book shows how AI redefines the GCC value equation, moving it far beyond labour cost savings.
